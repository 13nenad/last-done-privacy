Privacy Policy — Last Done

How Last Done handles information when you use the app.

Effective date: 12 October 2026

App: Last Done

Developer: Last Done app developer

Privacy contact: nenadsrbinovski13@gmail.com

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Last Done (“the App”) handles information when you use the application.

Last Done helps you track household tasks, maintenance activities and other recurring activities by recording when they were last completed, when they are due, and when reminders should be sent.

The App is designed to store your tracker information locally on your device. The App also integrates Google AdMob for advertising, which may collect and process certain device and usage information as described below.

Where consent is required by applicable law or platform policy, the relevant processing will be subject to the applicable consent choices.

2. Information stored on your device

The App may store information you enter or configure, including:

  • Tracker names and descriptions.
  • Task completion dates and times.
  • Repeat intervals and due-date settings.
  • Reminder times and related notification settings.
  • Other information needed to operate your trackers.

This information is stored locally using the App’s Room database and related Android storage mechanisms. The App developer does not operate a server to collect or synchronise this tracker information.

Tracker information is used to provide the App’s core functionality, including displaying tracker status, calculating when tasks are due and scheduling reminders.

Anyone who can access your unlocked device or the App’s local data may potentially be able to access information stored there. Use your device’s security features to protect your information.

3. Notifications and reminders

If you configure reminders, the App may schedule local notifications using Android notification and scheduling features.

Your reminder settings are used to determine when notifications should be scheduled. Notification delivery may depend on your device settings, notification permissions, battery optimisation, operating-system behaviour and other device conditions.

The App does not need to send your tracker information to its own server to schedule local reminders.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements and support the continued development of the App.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, depending on the SDK version, configuration, advertising features used, device settings and consent choices. This information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device’s general location.
  • Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers.
  • Information about interactions with the App and advertisements.
  • Diagnostic, performance and related technical information.

Google and relevant advertising partners may use information for purposes such as delivering and measuring advertisements, analytics, preventing fraud and abuse, and maintaining advertising services. Depending on the configuration and consent choices, advertisements may be personalised or non-personalised.

Your tracker records are stored locally by the App and are not intentionally sent to AdMob as part of the App’s tracker functionality. AdMob may independently collect device and advertising-related information through its SDK.

For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

5. In-app purchases

The App may offer a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing to remove advertisements.

Purchase transactions are processed through Google Play. Google may process account, transaction and payment-related information under its own terms and privacy policies.

The App may receive purchase-status information necessary to determine whether the Remove Ads feature should be enabled and to support purchase restoration, where implemented. The developer does not receive your full payment card details through the standard Google Play Billing purchase flow.

For more information, review Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Information sharing

The App developer does not operate a backend service for collecting or sharing your locally stored tracker records.

Information may nevertheless be processed by third parties when their services are used by the App:

  • Google AdMob: May receive and process advertising-related, device, diagnostic and usage information through its SDK, as described in this policy.
  • Google Play: Processes app distribution and, where applicable, purchase transactions and purchase status.
  • Android and device service providers: The operating system may process information necessary to provide notifications, device functionality, app installation and related services.

These providers handle information under their own applicable privacy policies and terms. Their processing may occur on systems located outside your country of residence.

The developer does not sell your tracker records or intentionally disclose them to advertising providers.

7. Data retention and deletion

Tracker information remains stored locally on your device until you delete it within the App, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App, subject to any applicable operating-system backup or restoration behaviour.

If the App provides a delete-tracker function, deleting a tracker is intended to remove that record from the App’s local database.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its local app data, subject to Android backup, device-transfer or restoration features that may apply. Reinstalling or restoring a device may affect whether previously stored data is available.

Advertising and purchase-related information handled by Google is subject to Google’s applicable retention and deletion practices.

Because the App does not operate a user-account system or a developer-operated server containing tracker records, the developer generally has no central copy of those records to retrieve or delete.

8. Security

The App uses local device storage for tracker information and relies on Android’s application security mechanisms to help isolate app data.

Information transmitted by third-party services, such as AdMob and Google Play, is subject to the security measures used by those services. Google Mobile Ads SDK data transmission and security practices are described in Google’s documentation.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device with appropriate security settings and keep the App and operating system up to date.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The developer does not knowingly request children’s personal information directly through the tracker functionality. However, third-party advertising services may process device or usage information. The App’s declared target audience, advertising configuration and any applicable child-directed treatment must reflect its actual intended users and implementation.

If you believe information has been provided inappropriately, contact the developer using the email address below.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can manage tracker information through the App’s available controls and your device settings. You can also manage notification permissions and, where available, advertising privacy settings through your device or Google’s services.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights under applicable law, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal information held by a service provider.

For questions or privacy requests relating to the App, contact the developer at nenadsrbinovski13@gmail.com. Requests concerning information held by Google may also need to be directed to Google.
