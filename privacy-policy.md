## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Last Done ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

Last Done helps you track household tasks, maintenance activities and other recurring activities by recording when they were last completed, when they are due, and when reminders should be sent.

The App is designed to store your tracker information locally on your device. However, the App also integrates Google AdMob for advertising, and these advertising services may collect and process certain device and usage information as described below.

By using the App, you can review this policy to understand how information is handled. Where consent is required by applicable law or platform policy, the relevant processing will be subject to the applicable consent choices.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The App may store information you enter or configure, including:

* Tracker names and descriptions.
* Task completion dates and times.
* Repeat intervals and due-date settings.
* Reminder times and related notification settings.
* Other information needed to operate your trackers.

This information is stored locally using the App's Room database and related Android storage mechanisms. The App does not operate its own server to collect or synchronise this tracker information.

Tracker information is used to provide the App's core functionality, including displaying tracker status, calculating when tasks are due and scheduling reminders.

Anyone who can access your unlocked device or the App's local data may potentially be able to access information stored there. Please use your device's security features to protect your information.

## 3. Notifications and reminders

If you configure reminders, the App may schedule local notifications using Android notification and scheduling features.

Your reminder settings are used to determine when notifications should be scheduled. The notifications are intended to remind you about activities you have configured in the App.

Notification delivery may depend on your device settings, notification permissions, battery optimisation, operating-system behaviour and other device conditions.

The App does not need to send your tracker information to its own server to schedule local reminders.

## 4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements and support the continued development of the App.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, including:

* IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location.
* Advertising identifiers and other device or app identifiers.
* Information about interactions with the App and advertisements.
* Diagnostic, performance and related technical information.

Google and its relevant advertising partners may use information for purposes such as delivering and measuring advertisements, analytics, preventing fraud and abuse, and maintaining advertising services. The information processed can depend on the SDK version, configuration, advertising features used, your device settings and your consent choices.

Where applicable, advertising may be personalised or non-personalised depending on the relevant settings and consent choices. You may be able to manage advertising privacy settings through your Android device and Google's available advertising controls.

Your tracker records are stored locally by the App and are not intentionally sent to AdMob by the App as part of its tracker functionality. However, AdMob may independently collect device and advertising-related information through its SDK.

For further information, please review Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google's information about how the Mobile Ads SDK handles data is available at:

https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

The App may receive purchase status information necessary to determine whether the Remove Ads feature should be enabled and to support purchase restoration, where implemented.

The App developer does not receive your full payment card details through the standard Google Play Billing purchase flow.

For information about Google's handling of purchase-related information, please review Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Information sharing

The App developer does not operate a backend service for collecting or sharing your locally stored tracker records.

Information may nevertheless be processed by third parties when their services are used by the App:

* **Google AdMob:** Receives and processes advertising-related, device, diagnostic and usage information through its SDK, as described in this policy.
* **Google Play:** Processes app distribution and, where applicable, purchase transactions and purchase status.
* **Android and device service providers:** The operating system may process information necessary to provide notifications, device functionality, app installation and related services.

These providers handle information under their own applicable privacy policies and terms. Their processing may occur on systems located outside your country of residence.

The developer does not sell your tracker records or intentionally disclose them to advertising providers.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Tracker information remains stored locally on your device until you delete it within the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App, subject to any applicable operating-system backup or restoration behaviour.

If the App provides a delete-tracker function, deleting a tracker is intended to remove that record from the App's local database.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its local app data, subject to Android backup, device-transfer or restoration features that may apply. Reinstalling or restoring a device may therefore affect whether previously stored data is available.

Advertising and purchase-related information handled by Google is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices.

Because the App does not operate a user-account system or a developer-operated server containing tracker records, the developer generally has no central copy of those records to retrieve or delete.

## 8. Security

The App uses local device storage for tracker information and relies on Android's application security mechanisms to help isolate app data.

Information transmitted by third-party services, such as AdMob and Google Play, is subject to the security measures used by those services. Google Mobile Ads SDK data is transmitted using encryption in transit as described in Google's documentation.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You should protect your device with appropriate security settings and keep the App and operating system up to date.
